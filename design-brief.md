# بيت الرياضة (Bayt Al-Riyada) design brief

Design read: shoppers on phones in the Arab world who want sneakers for the court and leather for the office from one trusted shop. Register: bold, athletic, premium, fast.

Concept spine: "from the pitch to the office". One shoe transforms from a running sneaker into a polished oxford while the visitor scrolls; the whole page is that crossing.

Delivery tier: cinema (scroll-scrub film is the Tier-1 mechanic), RTL Arabic first, mobile first.

Animation mode: animated-website

Journey shape: single-shot (one continuous film, sneaker morphs into oxford, centered subject on a cobalt studio void).
Journey: one chapter "من الملعب إلى المكتب" held over the whole film (focal point: the shoe at center), tags: أحذية رياضية, أحذية رسمية, وصل حديثاً. Then content sections: brands marquee, two worlds (sport / formal), live collection with filters and 3D tilt cards, services, visit.
World grammar: deep cobalt void #0B2A9E fading to #061653, soft rim light, floor reflection, white #F5F7FF, sun yellow #FFC93C accent.
Mobile framing: subject center-safe; copy docks to the bottom on narrow screens; 720p mobile encode.
Delivery budget: desktop clip <= 32 MiB, mobile clip <= 16 MiB.

Locked palette: cobalt #0B2A9E, deep navy #061653, panel #0E2380, white #F5F7FF, muted #B8C4F5, sun yellow #FFC93C. Defense: saturated cobalt with a sun-yellow signal reads as sport kit and stays far from the banned near-black plus orange/neon families.
Locked type: Readex Pro (display, Arabic + Latin) with IBM Plex Sans Arabic (body). No serif.

Section plan (no consecutive repeats):
1. Scroll-scrub hero journey (full-bleed film, overlay copy)
2. Brands marquee (two opposing tickers)
3. Two worlds (split diptych panels, 3D tilt)
4. Collection (filter chips + tilt product grid, data from the store database)
5. Services (three-up icon rail)
6. Visit and contact (info block + WhatsApp)
7. Footer

Asset plan: generated storyboard, six product shots, one hero film (10s), SVG shoe mark + head kit, OG cover.

CTA inventory: nav "تسوق الآن" (outline glow fill swap), hero "تسوق المجموعة" (sliding arrow chip), hero "زيارة المحل" (drawing underline), product "اطلب عبر واتساب" (pulsing glyph pill), worlds "استعرض" (corner arrow flip).
